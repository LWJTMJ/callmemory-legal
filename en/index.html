Call Your Memory — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-12　|　Applies to version 1.0 and later

Effective date: 12 November 2026
Applies to: Call Your Memory / 打给回忆 (iOS), version 1.0 and later
Developer: Weijie Li (李杰)
Contact: lwjtmj@outlook.com

The short version: this app has no server and no account. Your recordings stay on your device and in your own iCloud.

1. Who handles your data

The app is published and maintained by an independent developer. We do not run a backend server. There is no sign-up, no login and no account of any kind, and nothing is uploaded to any system we control.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not collect identity information — no name, email address, phone number or ID — because there is no account to attach it to.
  • We do not collect device identifiers for tracking, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not send usage events, crash logs or diagnostics to our servers.
  • We do not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or social-login SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade data. There is no data to sell.

3. Microphone

  • Why: to record what you leave for a day, or for a person.
  • When: the microphone is requested and used only after you press the record key. Nothing is captured while you are not recording.
  • Where it goes: recordings are written to the app's private storage on your device. If iCloud sync is available (your device is signed in to iCloud), the same recording is also written to your own private CloudKit database.
  • What we do with it: nothing. We do not upload, listen to, transcribe or analyse recordings, and we do not use them for model training. The developer cannot access your private iCloud database.

4. Contacts (read-only)

  • Why: to match a phone number to a person, so that when you dial someone the answering machine can greet you by their name and file what you leave under them.
  • What is read: given name, family name, nickname and phone numbers. Not email addresses, postal addresses, birthdays, notes or photos.
  • Where it goes: it stays in the app's private storage on your device, used for local matching only.
  • What we never do: we do not write to, modify or delete your contacts, and we do not upload them.
  • You can decline: declining access only disables "dial a person". Dialling a date, recording and future messages keep working.

5. Location (optional, never requested by default)

  • Why: only if you switch on the optional "city name" setting, the app reads your approximate city to word on-screen operator and incoming-call copy.
  • Accuracy and timing: approximate (about 3 km), and only while you are using the app.
  • Not stored, not uploaded: coordinates are never written to disk and never leave the device. The app keeps only the city name, and gives up if the request takes longer than about 2.5 seconds.
  • Dialling never triggers location: typing any number on the in-app keypad does not request location.
  • You can turn it off: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Call Your Memory.

6. Notifications

  • Why: to remind you when a future message you scheduled has arrived.
  • When requested: only when you create a future message.
  • Content: notifications are generated and delivered locally by the system and never pass through a server. The title is fixed; the body contains only the title you gave that message.
  • You can turn it off: iOS Settings → Notifications → Call Your Memory.

7. iCloud (private CloudKit database)

  • What is stored: your recordings and their metadata (title, note, recording date, creation time), in the CloudKit private database iCloud.com.jayli.callmemory.
  • Who can see it: only your own Apple ID can read or write it. By Apple's design a private database is not readable by the developer; we cannot view, export or use its contents.
  • Without iCloud: nothing syncs, and recordings exist only on the device.
  • Operator: iCloud is provided and hosted by Apple, and Apple's privacy policy also applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

8. Purchases and membership

  • Purchases go through Apple's In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Pricing, payment method, billing and refunds are handled entirely by Apple.
  • We never see payment information — not your name, card number or billing address, and not how you paid. The app knows exactly one thing: whether the Apple ID on this device currently holds the membership entitlement.
  • The free-call count and this month's usage are stored only in the device Keychain. They are never sent anywhere and disappear when the app is deleted.

9. Retention, deletion and export

  • On the device: deleting a recording in the app, or deleting the app, removes the corresponding recordings and settings.
  • In iCloud: deleting the app does not remove the iCloud copy. You can delete this app's data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, or delete recordings in the app and let that deletion sync.
  • Export: you may export all recordings as an archive to the system Files app (iCloud Drive or anywhere else). Once exported, the files are yours and this policy no longer covers them.
  • There is no "ask the developer to delete my data" process, because we never had your data to begin with.

10. Third parties

  • No third-party data SDKs at all: no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no social login.
  • Only Apple system frameworks are used: CloudKit (iCloud sync), StoreKit (purchases), AVFoundation and system speech synthesis (recording and voice playback), CoreLocation (optional city name), Contacts (read-only), UserNotifications (local notifications).
  • Some bundled sound effects come from ZapSplat (standard licence) and CC0 sources, credited in-app under Settings → "Sources & notes". They are audio files shipped inside the app and involve no data processing.

11. Children

The app is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because there is no upload, no account and no social feature, anything a child records stays on the device and in that user's own iCloud.

12. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that needs the network or collects data, we will update this policy before that update ships and say so in the app or on the store page. Material changes to how data is handled will ask for your consent again.

13. Contact
